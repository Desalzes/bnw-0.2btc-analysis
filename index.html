Tools for the 0.2 BTC "Brave New World" puzzle

Browser tools for working on the picture and checking ideas against the published record. They need no install and no account, and everything runs on your own machine.

Picture lab

Zoom in to the pixel, split the colour channels, stretch faint marks, pull up the pale wash, flip and turn the picture, and unwrap the seal and the clock into straight strips.

Clock overlay

Lay the painted clock over the whole picture, move and turn it by hand, and see which labelled items land in which slice.

Was it tried?

Search a word or a passphrase against the published search configs and the run ledger.

Try a phrase

Type a phrase and compare its addresses with the prize address, here in the browser.

What these pages do

  • Each page is a static file. Once it has loaded, nothing you type or do leaves your browser.
  • The pages only read and compare. None of them can build, sign or send a transaction.
  • The lookup covers the published record, which ends on 2 October 2026. Later searches are not in it.

The full write-up, the search engine and the run ledger are in the GitHub repository.
